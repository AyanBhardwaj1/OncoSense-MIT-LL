Connect your OncoSense Band

Plug the band into this computer and watch, live, how your skin returns light: hydration, surface changes, pigment, and the band’s own neural network reading.

  1. Flash firmware v3 from firmware/OncoSense with the Arduino IDE.
  2. Plug the band in over USB-C and wear it on your inner forearm.
  3. Choose the port after pressing Connect band. It is usually named “QT Py” or “USB Serial”.
